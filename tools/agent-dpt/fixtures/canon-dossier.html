The mechanism

Late-cycle hens over-consume crude protein because rations are formulated for the flock’s mean, not its age curve. Shifting the last 12 weeks to synthetic lysine and methionine micro-dosing reduces nitrogen excretion by 18–24%3 while holding egg mass, and the lower ammonia load cuts ventilation energy demand2 in enclosed houses.

The council’s economic case rests on the spread between crude soy protein and synthetic amino acids, which has narrowed 31% since 20234. Feed is 62% of a layer operation’s cost; the intervention touches only the formulation software, not the barn.
