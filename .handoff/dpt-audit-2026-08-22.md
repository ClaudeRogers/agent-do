# DPT Full Audit — 2026-08-22

**Question asked:** Is dpt doing what was intended (the moon), and is it doing harm (mistaking fingers for the moon)? The moon, per Erik: silicon intelligence with native vision in its own latent space, producing real, award-level, never-repeated designs — the kind of quality recognitionoracle.com exemplifies. Immediate stakes: the Palingenesis redesign (5 lanes, ratified editorial canon) is about to launch with dpt wired into a live PostToolUse hook.

**Method:** Full read of every rule (all 5 layers + synthesis + report generator + hook + dispatcher, 4,537-line engine, dist verified == fresh build of src), then empirical falsification: five controlled fixtures scored at 1440×900 — two honest implementations of the ratified Palingenesis canon (Today, Dossier), one disciplined neon-template anti-canon, one deliberately broken page, one oklch color-space probe — plus recognitionoracle.com scored live. Fixtures, scan JSONs, and screenshots preserved in the session scratchpad (`dpt-fixtures/`, `scan-*.json`, `shot-*.png`).

---

## Verdict

**DPT is a working craft-floor detector wearing the costume of a design-quality meter.** The floor half is real and valuable: the deliberately broken page scored 29 F with four correctly-named critical failures. The quality half does not exist: the ratified canon Dossier scored **67** and the explicitly banned neon-template page scored **66** — a one-point tie between the aesthetic the north star canonizes and the aesthetic it bans. Erik's own exemplar of the goal, recognitionoracle.com, scored **70 B-**, and its fix list orders the removal of every signature move the site has.

The tagline is honest — "DPT does not measure taste. It measures whether the unconscious will flinch" — but the score, the letter grades, the hook, and the fix loop are all wired as if it measured taste. That gap is the finger mistaken for the moon.

## Structural biases (why canon loses and templates coast)

- **Prose pages face rules card dashboards never meet.** The barf page contains no `<p>` at all → ts03 (measure), sr06 (blowout), sr09 (margins) all pass on n=0, while canon prose is fully policed. The typography layer audits the pages canon builds and waves through the genre canon bans.
- **Uniformity is scored as coherence; range is scored as chaos.** Barf's two font sizes scored **0.925** type coherence; the canon's Perfect-Fourth ladder plus UI sizes scored **0.4**. Barf's overall structural coherence: 91 — the highest of all six pages. Poverty reads as discipline to a variance metric.
- **The measure rule is miscalibrated for serif faces.** ts03 estimates chars/line as width ÷ (fontSize × 0.48) (typographic-skeleton.js:114); the ratified 65ch Georgia column measures "83 chars" → violation. The canonical measure fails the measure rule by coefficient error.
- **`sr08` whitespace balance is vacuous or inverted.** Any centered `max-width` shell under ~98% viewport width fills the 4×4 density grid → balance 1.0 regardless of actual whitespace (spatial-rhythm.js:440 skip heuristic); a narrow editorial column scores 0 (worst). It literally rewards wall-to-wall layouts — the inverse of "whitespace is the primary ornament."
- **Viewport-only vision.** Most checks filter to the first 900px. The Dossier's economics table, council, and numeral craft were never evaluated — ts18 (tabular numerals, the canon's numeral law) reported 0 cells checked. dpt scored the hero and called it the page.
- **Everything below `document.fonts` and `rgb()` is invisible.** parseColor reads only rgb/rgba (utils.js:8-18). The oklch probe — Tailwind v4's default color space — scored **97 chromatic with "100% contrast pass rate" listed as a strength** while carrying a deliberate 1.6:1 contrast violation, and earned a phantom "no CTA" critical for a button whose oklch background the parser returned null on. On the redesign's likely stack, the chromatic layer reports silent perfection. Gradients/background-images are likewise unseen; `getEffectiveBackground` falls through them to a default white, which both misses real neon garishness and can manufacture phantom contrast failures under text-over-gradient.

## Mechanical defects (fix regardless of philosophy)

1. **Rule-count claims are false three ways:** help/README/registry say 72, dpt-report footer says "70+", the engine emits **65** checks (12 CF + 22 TS + 10 SR + 13 AA + 8 CO; counted from scan output). Of those, ~50 influence the score; **9 checks appear in the `violations` fix list but have zero score effect** (ts10, ts11, ts12, ts15, ts16, ts18, ts20, sr10, aa05) — the loop chases them, the number can't move.
2. **`violations` is not "sorted by impact"** as help/README/CLAUDE.md claim — it iterates layers in fixed order, no impact computation (bin/agent-dpt:316-326). And **cf01 contrast — the highest-weighted rule — never appears in it** (it emits no `pass` field, and the filter requires `pass: false`). The fix list omits the most important fixable thing and leads with unfixable trivia.
3. **`/tmp/dpt-baseline.json` is a global singleton** (bin/agent-dpt:69) — not per-session, not per-URL. Three Wave-B lanes running `baseline`/`diff` concurrently silently clobber each other's comparison points.
4. **ts06 disagrees with its own synthesis:** the check passes ≤3 families (typographic-skeleton.js:213) but synthesis scores 3 families as 50/100 (synthesis.js:133). The ratified Source trio (serif+sans+mono) is taxed for existing.
5. **README cites a `research/` directory that does not exist** anywhere in the repo (README.md:122-132: "48 books", "17 new rules", Sillence/Bechara citations). Every threshold in the engine is a bare literal whose claimed receipts are absent — in this repo's own doctrine, numbers without an authority. The perceptual reasoning may well be real; the receipts didn't ship.
6. **dpt-report hard-codes a hallucination** (bin/dpt-report:772): whenever >50% of targets are undersized it prints "The sidebar links at 32px are the primary offenders" — sidebar or no sidebar, 32px or not.
7. **Hook drift + misattribution:** the registered `~/.claude/hooks/dpt-post-edit.sh` is a stale full copy diverging from the repo version (dpt's install.sh copies instead of thin-wrapping like the other agent-do hooks). And the hook scores **whatever page the daemon has open** — during this audit it fired five times on fixture writes and injected scores from an unrelated app's `/home` page. It does append the scored URL (good), but the score still lands formatted as feedback on the edit.
8. **co03 component consistency defaults to 0.4 when no class-groups repeat** (coherence.js:291) — utility-class CSS (Tailwind, the likely stack) rarely yields ≥3 identical class strings, so the redesign eats a low coherence input for using modern CSS conventions.

## What dpt genuinely is — and the real moon

Strip the costume and what remains is valuable: **deterministic, instant (7–11ms), zero-cost proprioception.** It knows where the bones are: contrast ratios, font sizes, target geometry, token discipline, animation bounds. The broken-page result proves the flinch-floor works. Several rules are even canon-aligned: tinted greys, halation, pure-black body text, saturation containment, destructive-as-primary, animation ≤200ms, transition specificity.

But the stated moon — native vision in silicon's own latent space, producing novel award-level beauty every time — has a requirement dpt structurally cannot meet: **a metric that steers generation must be style-invariant, or it manufactures a monoculture.** dpt's style rules (one filled CTA, 2 families, 2–3× hero, 4–6 nav items, uniform density, 44px everything) encode 2010s-SaaS conventions as law. Optimized, they converge every design toward the same attractor — the exact opposite of never-the-same-twice. Meanwhile the actual latent-space vision already exists in the stack: the model looking at the screenshot (`browse screenshot` + Read, Erik's own visual-truth doctrine). dpt was never the eye; it's the inner ear.

**The honest architecture:** floors from dpt (style-invariant flinch physics — is any text unreadable, any target untappable by pointer standards, any craft broken), taste from model-vision on rendered screenshots against the ratified canon, novelty from generation — and the score never used as a maximization target, only `diff` as a regression tripwire.
